## Proved scope

1. Definition and zero-set characterization of the dual-phase defect functional.
2. Similarity/rotation invariance, polarity symmetry, and Fourier representation.
3. The zero-phase symmetry constraint `Rot_{2α}K=K`.
4. Exact rectangle curvature and the critical aspect ratio.
5. Exact fourth derivative and quartic nondegeneracy at the critical point.
6. Local symmetric two-branch bifurcation with critical exponent `1/2`.
7. Uniform convergence to the degenerating-rectangle limit.
8. Hausdorff continuity of the phase defect and its minimum under a common interior-point condition.

## Open scope

Global uniqueness and monotonicity of the minimizing branch, global absorption at the critical ratio, the singular boundary-layer profile, and comprehensive bibliographic priority remain open.
