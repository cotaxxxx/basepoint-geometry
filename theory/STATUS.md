| Paper | Primary subject |
|---|---|
| P1 | Local prolate-spheroid structure |
| P2 | Oblate-spheroid structure, including boundary entry |
| P3 | Global prolate-spheroid structure |
| P4 | Triaxial-ellipsoid structure |

## Evidence boundary

Every claim retains the evidence class, derivation class, scope, and provenance assigned in its originating repository. Reorganization does not promote evidence.

## Research-note boundary

Multiple-body work begins with the unresolved definition problem of extending (E) to external base points. It remains a research note until definitions stabilize and theorem-level statements become available. Only then will separate publication be considered.
