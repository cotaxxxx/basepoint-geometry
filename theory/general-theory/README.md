# Basepoint Geometry: General Theory

**基点幾何論・総論**

## Purpose

The General Theory will identify and prove structures that do not depend on a single geometric family. It will be extracted from P1–P4 rather than imposed on them in advance.

## Provisional scope

- the basic data and framework ((P,\Lambda,E));
- stationary base points and stationary orbits;
- symmetry and equivariance;
- Morse and Morse–Bott structures;
- local reduction and bifurcation;
- stability under explicit nondegeneracy hypotheses;
- provenance, reconstruction, and claim scope.

## Drafting rule

During P1–P4, reusable statements are first recorded in the project-level general-theory seeds together with their actual hypotheses and fixed source references. They enter the General Theory paper only after their proof scope is clear.

## Timing

The General Theory paper is planned after P1–P4. A limited earlier feasibility check is permitted for the proposed stability theorem because its attainable hypotheses determine the eventual scope of the paper.
