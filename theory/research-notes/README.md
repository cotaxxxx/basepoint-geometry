# Basepoint Geometry: Research Notes

**基点幾何論・研究ノート**

## Status

**RESEARCH NOTES / NOT BINDING**

This directory is not presently scheduled as a paper. Separate publication will be considered only after the definitions stabilize and theorem-level statements become available.

## Subject boundary

The notes are reserved for configurations involving **multiple geometric bodies**. Single-body stretching, compression, deformation, bifurcation, unfolding, and polyhedral examples belong to Particular Studies.

## First problem: external base points

The first task is not a theorem but a definition problem:

> How should the basepoint energy (E) be extended when the relevant base point lies outside one or more bodies?

Before counting stationary points or proposing dynamics, the notes must determine:

- the admissible external-basepoint domain;
- the treatment of visibility, orientation, and sign;
- whether the cone-volume weighting remains a probability measure;
- continuity at body boundaries;
- invariance and normalization requirements;
- the relation between the single-body and multiple-body definitions.

Until these choices are fixed, numerical experiments remain exploratory and cannot support a theorem claim.

## Future questions

Only after the external-basepoint definition is stable may the notes investigate interaction between bodies, coupled stationary structures, or a possible connection to Basepoint Dynamics.
