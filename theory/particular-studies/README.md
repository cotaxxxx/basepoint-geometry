# Basepoint Geometry: Particular Studies

**基点幾何論・各論**

## Scope

Particular Studies contains P1–P4 and later investigations of **single geometric bodies**.

Its subject includes:

- spheres, prolate and oblate spheroids, and triaxial ellipsoids;
- smooth and nonsmooth single bodies;
- stretching and compression through geometric parameters;
- stationary-basepoint location, number, symmetry type, and Morse character;
- boundary entry and disappearance;
- stationary-orbit bifurcation;
- symmetry breaking and unfolding;
- polyhedra, frusta, and other single-body deformation models.

The governing distinction is not “quadratic versus nonquadratic” or “structure versus bifurcation.” If the problem concerns one body, it belongs here.

## Role in the theory

These papers establish concrete results first. Arguments that do not depend on the chosen body become candidates for the General Theory.

## Evidence boundary

Implementations, diagnostics, and certificates remain in the originating experimental repositories. High-precision agreement is not a certified enclosure, and citation or relocation does not promote a claim.
