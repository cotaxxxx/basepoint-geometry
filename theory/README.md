| Division | Japanese | Role |
|---|---|---|
| [General Theory](general-theory/README.md) | 総論 | General theory paper, to be written after P1–P4 |

| [Research Notes](research-notes/README.md) | 研究ノート | Multiple-body questions and undeveloped definitions; NOT_BINDING |

There is no “Particular Studies II” in the present publication plan.

## Organizing boundary

The primary division is **single body / multiple bodies**.

- Particular Studies treats one geometric body under stretching, compression, deformation, symmetry breaking, bifurcation, and unfolding.
- Research Notes treats configurations involving multiple bodies. This area begins with the problem of extending the energy (E) to external base points.

This boundary avoids assigning one single-body result to different divisions merely because it concerns both a geometric family and a bifurcation.

## Research hierarchy

1. complete the particular papers P1–P4;
2. extract their common structures into the General Theory;
3. use Basepoint Geometry as the foundation for future Basepoint Dynamics.

Basepoint Dynamics is the long-term destination, not a claim of the present theory workspace.
